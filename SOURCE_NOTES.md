# Source notes

The central source registry is `content.sources` in [`src/content.js`](src/content.js). No claim is made that the game’s language or cultural specifics have been independently verified.

## Access record

During development on 2026-10-14, an HTTPS request to `https://ojibwe.lib.umn.edu/` returned a proxy **403 Forbidden**. The earlier prototype also failed to access a dictionary entry. No entry text, community metadata, audio, or dialect information was available to validate candidate words. Accordingly, **only English vocabulary is displayed** in the adventure. Candidate spellings are explicitly unverified review items.

The Ojibwe People’s Dictionary is a collaborative source with speaker-specific entries. Once accessible, record exact entries and their speaker/dialect context, rather than assigning one dialect label to all words. Prefer local tribal language sources or partner-approved materials when available. Local education partners have not yet been contacted or involved.

## What is and is not sourced

- The title, fictional time travel, characters, puzzles, and dialogue are original game writing.
- The seven English teaching names and seasonal learning topics come from the user’s project specification. Interpretations are original classroom wording awaiting local review, not sourced Elder quotations or definitive cultural instruction.
- General weather, observation, materials, habitat, and teamwork ideas are conservative educational framing. Before classroom use, verify refinements with appropriate local sources. Avoid adding detailed harvesting/processing practices without verification.
- Potential supplementary science/safety sources are University of Minnesota Extension (`https://extension.umn.edu/`) and Minnesota DNR (`https://www.dnr.state.mn.us/`); access and specific pages have not been verified in this task. These sources do not substitute for local cultural/language review.

No traditional story, ceremony, sacred quote, animal association, or fake pronunciation audio is included. All unresolved language and practice questions are listed in `CONTENT_REVIEW.md`.
